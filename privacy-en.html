This Privacy Policy sets out how the app "GPS TA" handles personal information and user information.

1. Information we collect

- Account information (the email address obtained from Sign in with Apple / Google; if you use Apple's "Hide My Email", this is the anonymous relay address Apple issues)
- Your public name (shown to other users, e.g. as the author of a course you post): the nickname you set, or, if you haven't set one, an anonymous name the App generates automatically (e.g. "Driver1234"). We never use your real name from Sign in with Apple / Google as this public name. We do not store your real (full) name from Apple on our servers
- Shared course information you publish (name, location, path, distance, duration)
- Parking spots you register, and what you post to their message board
- Stamps you place on the map (accident/roadwork locations; these stop being shown 3 hours after posting)
- The content of reports you file, and the list of users you have blocked (used to act on inappropriate posts)
- Location data while timing a run (your GPS track). This is saved only on your device, as a record, and is never sent to our servers
- Your purchase and subscription status for the paid plan (GPS TA Pro), via RevenueCat (see 5)
- Favorites (★) records: that you added or removed a ★ on a published course, and when. The App generates a random ID for each device (not an identifier built into the device); our server converts it into a value that cannot be turned back (a hash) and records only that value. Favorites are recorded even if you are not signed in, but they are not linked to your account or to you personally
- Information for receiving favorite notifications (only if you turn them on): the notification address (push token), the device type (iOS / Android), the device's time zone, the App's display language, your on/off setting, when you last switched it, when the device last registered, and when we last sent you a notification. These are stored linked to the account you are signed in with
We do not currently use any dedicated crash-reporting or analytics tool. If we introduce one in the future, we will update this policy.

2. How it is used

- To provide the App's features (signing in, sharing and displaying courses and parking info, timing, showing weather)
- To act on inappropriate posts (reviewing reports, providing the block feature)
- To prevent misuse and to improve quality
- To show the publisher of a course how many ★ it has received, and to let them know by push notification when it is favorited (we never show who added a ★; the device's time zone is used so that notifications are not sent at night)

3. Location

Location is used to create routes, measure times, show your current position, and find nearby shared courses and parking spots. Your timing track is saved only on your device and is never sent elsewhere. When you publish a course or register a parking spot, the App checks on your device how far your current location is from the course or spot, to confirm you are actually there (the current location used for this check is not sent to our servers; the location of the course or spot you publish or register is). A map stamp sends the location of the point you choose on the map (the centre of the map); placing a stamp does not check your current location. You can change the permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Where information goes / third-party services

To the extent needed to provide the App's features, we send information to the following external services, keeping any identifying information to the minimum necessary:
- Supabase (stores course, parking and message-board data; backs sign-in)
- RevenueCat (manages purchases and subscription status for the paid plan; see 5)
- Apple / Google (authentication for Sign in with Apple / Google)
- OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place search and reverse geocoding; sends your search text and location)
- OSRM (the public routing servers at router.project-osrm.org and routing.openstreetmap.de; sends the coordinates of your start, waypoints and destination to calculate a route)
- Open-Meteo (sends the coordinates of a chosen location to fetch its weather and temperature)
- Apple Maps (iOS) / Google Maps (Android) (map display)
- Expo (Expo Push Service; to deliver favorite notifications, we send the notification address and the notification content (the course name and the number of people). Expo delivers them to your device through Apple Push Notification service (iOS) / Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android))
Other than as described above, we do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent, except where required by law.
The operator and country of each of these services are listed in 8.

5. Purchase information

RevenueCat is used to handle purchases and manage subscription status. The purchase information issued by the store (the product purchased, the subscription expiry date, an anonymous user ID and so on) is sent to them. Payment details such as credit card numbers are handled directly by the store, and are never obtained by the App or by RevenueCat.

6. Retention

- Records and routes saved on your device stay there until you delete them
- Courses you published, parking spots you registered, board posts and map stamps stay on our servers until you delete them or delete your account (a map stamp stops being shown 3 hours after posting, but the underlying record is not bound by that)
- Report records may be kept for up to one year after account deletion, to allow us to act on inappropriate posts (the link to the user who sent the report is removed when that account is deleted)
- Your block list is kept until you delete your account
- Favorites records are kept until the course is deleted. The record of a removed ★ is deleted 30 days after removal. A marker showing that a device's ★ from an earlier version were sent once in bulk (the hashed value only) is kept without a time limit so that they are not counted twice
- Information for receiving favorite notifications is deleted when you sign out, delete your account, or when we find that the notification address no longer works (for example, when we try to send a notification after you have deleted the App). If you turn notifications off, we stop sending them but keep the address
- The random per-device ID is stored on your device and disappears when you delete the App (on Android, it may come back if you restore the device from a backup)

7. Deleting your data

From "Delete account" on the settings screen, you can delete your posts (published courses, parking spots, board posts, map stamps), your profile, your block list, and the account itself. If you hold a paid plan, deleting your account does not automatically cancel the store subscription — cancel it separately from your store account settings. Purchase and subscription records for the paid plan (tied to an anonymous user ID managed by RevenueCat) are not deleted when you delete your account. To request their deletion, contact us at the address in section 12. Favorites records are not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not delete them. The record of a removed ★ is deleted 30 days after removal. Deleting the App deletes the per-device ID, so ★ added before that can no longer be removed from your side. Information for receiving favorite notifications is deleted when you sign out or delete your account (signing out deletes it for all of that account's devices).

8. Transfers to third parties outside Japan and outsourcing

Of the external services in 4, the following are run by businesses outside Japan (operator and country in brackets):
- Supabase (Supabase, Inc. / United States): we entrust it with handling personal data, as the store for course, parking and message-board data and as the basis for sign-in. Country where the servers storing the data are located: Japan (Tokyo region)
- RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc. / United States): we entrust it with managing purchases and subscription status for the paid plan
- Apple (Apple Inc. / United States), Google (Google LLC / United States): sign-in authentication, map display, delivery of push notifications (Apple Push Notification service, Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- OpenStreetMap Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation / United Kingdom): place search and reverse geocoding
- OSRM: routing.openstreetmap.de (FOSSGIS e.V. / Germany), router.project-osrm.org (Project OSRM demo server, provided by FOSSGIS e.V. / Germany): route calculation
- Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH / Switzerland): weather and temperature
- Expo (650 Industries, Inc. / United States): delivery of favorite notifications
For place search, route calculation, weather and map display, your search text and coordinates are sent directly from your device to each service, without passing through our servers.

Information on the personal-data protection systems of other countries is available in the survey of such systems published by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission. In outline:
- United States: there is no comprehensive federal privacy law; personal information is protected by sector-specific laws such as the Federal Trade Commission Act and by state laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act)
- United Kingdom and EU member states (including Germany): designated by the Commission's rules as countries whose systems for protecting personal information are at a level equivalent to Japan's
- Switzerland: has a comprehensive data protection law (the Federal Act on Data Protection)
We use the services we entrust with data after checking the measures each provider takes to protect personal information (the privacy policies and data processing terms each provider publishes), and on request we will provide information about those measures.

9. Security measures

To prevent leakage, loss or damage of the personal data we handle, we take the following main measures:
- Basic policy and rules: we have set out this policy and follow it at each stage of collecting, using, storing and deleting data
- Organisational and personnel measures: only the operator handles personal data, and if we become aware of a leak or similar incident we report it to the Personal Information Protection Commission and notify the people affected, as required by law
- Physical measures: devices used for administration are protected by screen locks and similar means so that others cannot operate them, and are guarded against theft and loss
- Technical measures: communications are encrypted (HTTPS). Access controls are in place for data on the server
- Understanding the external environment: where personal data is handled through services of businesses outside Japan, we take security measures with an understanding of that country's system for protecting personal information, as described in 8

10. Use by minors

If you are a minor, use the App, including creating an account, publishing courses or posts and buying the paid plan, only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If a parent or guardian finds that a minor's personal information was provided without their consent, please contact us at the address in 12; after the necessary checks, we will delete it or take other appropriate action.

You can ask us to notify you of the purpose of use of the personal data we hold about you, to disclose it (including records of provision to third parties), to correct, add to or delete it, to stop using or erase it, or to stop providing it to third parties. You can also delete your posts, profile and account yourself from "Delete account" on the settings screen (see 7).
- How to make a request: email gpsta391@gmail.com with the subject "Request regarding personal data", stating what you are requesting and which account it concerns (the email address you sign in with and your public name)
- Identity check: we confirm that you are the user of the account by a suitable method, such as matching the registered email address. If an agent makes the request, we may ask for documents showing their authority
- Reply: after confirming your identity, we aim to reply within two weeks as a rule, by the method you specify (for example, providing the data electronically by email). If that method is difficult, we will tell you so and reply by another method. If the law does not allow us to comply with a request, we will tell you so and why without delay
- Fee: free of charge
