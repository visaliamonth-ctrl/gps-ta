This Privacy Policy sets out how the app "GPS TA" handles personal information and user information.

1. Information we collect

- Account information (the email address and display name obtained from your sign-in provider)
- The nickname you set
- Route information you publish (location, path, distance and so on)
- Location data needed for the App to work (where the device has granted permission)

2. How it is used

- To provide the App's features (signing in, sharing and displaying routes, timing)
- To prevent misuse and to improve quality

3. Location

Location is used to create routes and to measure times. You can change the permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Sharing with third parties

Except where required by law, we do not provide personal information to third parties without your consent. Supabase is used as the backend.

5. Purchase information

RevenueCat is used to handle purchases and manage subscription status. The purchase information issued by the store (the product purchased, the subscription expiry date, an anonymous user ID and so on) is sent to them. Payment details such as credit card numbers are handled directly by the store, and are never obtained by the App or by RevenueCat.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete your posts, your profile and your account from "Delete account" on the settings screen.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the provider of the App.
