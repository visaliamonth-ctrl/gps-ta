Delete in the app

Open the app, tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top left of the Home screen → “Delete account” → confirm with “Delete”. Your account is deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by email.

To: gpsta391@gmail.com
Subject: “GPS TA account deletion”
Body: the email address you signed in with (if you hid your email with Sign in with Apple, the relay address Apple created)

To confirm it is you, please send the request from the email address you signed in with. We delete your data within 30 days of receiving the request and let you know when it is done.

Data that is deleted

• Your account (email address, display name, and the link to Sign in with Apple / Google)
• Your profile
• Courses you published
• Parking spots you added and the posts on their boards
• Posts you wrote on parking boards
• Map stamps you placed
• Your list of blocked users

Data that is not deleted or is handled elsewhere

• Data on your device (lap records, saved routes, settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
• Purchases and subscriptions are managed by Google Play / the App Store. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it from the subscription settings of Google Play (or the App Store).
• Reports you submitted may be kept to deal with abuse. Email us above if you want them deleted.
