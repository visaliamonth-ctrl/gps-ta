日本語 · English

Delete in the app

Open the app, tap the gear icon (Settings) at the top left of the Home screen → “Delete account” → confirm with “Delete account”. Your account is deleted immediately.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request deletion by email.

To: gpsta391@gmail.com
Subject: “GPS TA account deletion”
Body: the email address you signed in with (if you hid your email with Sign in with Apple, the relay address Apple created)

To confirm it is you, please send the request from the email address you signed in with. We delete your data within 30 days of receiving the request and let you know when it is done.

Data that is deleted

• Your account: your email address, and the record we keep on our server that links you to your Apple / Google account (the ID that identifies that Apple / Google account)
• If you signed in with Google: the name on your Google account and the URL of your profile photo, received from Google
• If you signed in with Apple: the information used to disconnect the app from your Apple account (a token issued by Apple)
• Your profile (your public name and the type of device you use: iOS / Android)
• Courses you published
• Parking spots you added and the posts on their boards
• Posts you wrote on parking boards
• Map stamps you placed
• Your list of blocked users
• Information for receiving favorite notifications (the notification address and settings; signing out also deletes it for all of that account's devices)

Data that is not deleted or is handled elsewhere

• Data on your device (lap records, saved routes, settings) is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.
• Purchases and subscriptions are managed by Google Play / the App Store. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — cancel it from the subscription settings of Google Play (or the App Store). Purchase and subscription records tied to an anonymous user ID managed by RevenueCat (the service we use to manage subscription status) are not deleted when you delete your account either. To have them deleted, email us at the address above.
• The permission to sign in to this app that remains on the Apple / Google side is managed by Apple / Google. When you delete your account in the app, we also revoke this permission, but only if you signed in with Apple and the information for disconnecting from Apple (a token) is stored. In some cases, such as if you signed in with Apple before October 13, 2026 and have not signed in again since, this information is not stored and the permission cannot be revoked. Revoking can also fail, for example because of the connection. The app does not show whether it was revoked. If your account is deleted at your request by email, or if you signed in with Google, we do not revoke it. If you signed in with Apple, check with the steps below after deleting your account, and if this app is still listed, stop using Sign in with Apple with it. You can also remove the permission on the Google side with the steps below.
 On iPhone / iPad: Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple → this app (or its developer) → Delete, then follow the onscreen steps to confirm. On some versions of iOS, Sign in with Apple is under Sign-In & Security, and the button is called Stop Using Apple ID.
 Without an iPhone (on the web): sign in on account.apple.com → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → select this app and stop using Sign in with Apple.
 If you signed in with Google: on your Google Account's linked apps page (https://myaccount.google.com/linkedapps), select Sign in with Google → zqxriihfbfcgniuwcjta.supabase.co → See details → Stop using Sign in with Google → Confirm. This app appears in the list as “zqxriihfbfcgniuwcjta.supabase.co”.
• Reports you submitted may be kept for up to one year after your account is deleted, to allow us to act on inappropriate posts (the link to you as the sender is removed when your account is deleted). After deletion we can no longer tell who sent a report, so if you want your reports deleted as well, email us at the address above before you delete your account.
• Favorites (★) records are not linked to your account, so deleting your account does not delete them. The record of a removed ★ is deleted 30 days after removal. Deleting the App deletes the per-device ID, so ★ added before that can no longer be removed from your side.
