Price

The price of GPS TA Pro (monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time Lifetime purchase) is the price shown on the App Store / Google Play product page and on the purchase screen in the app (prices in Japan include consumption tax).

For plans with a free trial, the purchase screen shows the length of the trial and the amount charged after it ends.

Additional fees

Internet data charges for downloading and using the app are paid by the customer.

Payment method

Payment is made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play, using the payment method registered to your store account.

Payment timing

You are charged by the store when you complete the purchase.

Auto-renewing subscriptions are charged automatically at each renewal, for the same period and at the same price, until you cancel. If the plan has a free trial, the first charge is made when the trial ends. The exact timing of charges follows each store’s rules.

Delivery

Available immediately after the purchase is completed.

Subscription term and renewal

  • The monthly plan renews every month and the yearly plan every year.
  • Unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, the subscription renews at the same price.
  • Lifetime is a single payment and does not renew.

Cancellation, returns and refunds

  • You can cancel an auto-renewing subscription at any time in your store account settings (iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). After cancelling, you can keep using Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling mid-period does not give a refund for the remaining time.
  • Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds follow the refund rules of the store you bought from (Apple / Google). Please send refund requests to that store.

System requirements

  • iPhone: iOS 15.1 or later (runs in iPhone mode on iPad)
  • Android: Android 7.0 or later, on a device with Google Play
  • Location (GPS) must be available. Showing the map and creating or publishing routes require an internet connection.

See each store’s product page for the latest compatibility.
