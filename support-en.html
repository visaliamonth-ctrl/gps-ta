FAQ

Restoring a purchase (new phone or reinstall)
Tap “Restore” at the bottom of the purchase screen. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancelling a subscription
iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → GPS TA.
Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → GPS TA.
Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

Deleting your account
Use “Delete account” at the bottom of the app’s Settings. To request deletion without the app, see Delete your account and data.

Reporting objectionable content
Report a course from its detail sheet, or a board post by long-pressing it. You can also block the user. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Where can I use it?
Timing is for closed courses only — circuits, gymkhana venues and private land. On public roads, always obey traffic laws and speed limits.
